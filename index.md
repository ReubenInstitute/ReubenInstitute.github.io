# Reuben Institute

![](/@@/logo.png)

_**"obsession for accuracy"**_

Reuben Institute is a scholarly organization and publishing studio dedicated to disseminating
and publishing foundational Hebrew and Aramaic texts. Motivated by the prevalence of inaccuracies
in existing published editions, the Institute utilises ancient, public‑domain source materials to
produce meticulously curated content across **print**, **digital**, **audio**, and **video** formats.
By combining rigorous academic standards with modern technology, the Institute ensures that
historical traditions remain accessible and accurate for contemporary audiences.


<section markdown="1">
## Multimedia Series

We produce short-form multimedia video series for social media platforms. The videos feature text slides with dynamic highlighting that syncs with the audio, set against visual backgrounds that range from atmospheric images to full video scenes. To produce these series, we use generative AI for our media assets: ElevenLabs for voice cloning and voice generation, Midjourney for image and video generation, and Suno for background music tracks.

[**Daily Psalm:**](media/PsalmsSeries.md) A psalm a day, verse by verse, accompanied by the Institute's serene fog‑and‑voice aesthetic and the now‑iconic baritone cloned from a 1976 recording. It's less a video series and more a quiet, daily moment of immersion — ancient words, simply presented.

[**Weekly Parashah:**](media/ParashahSeries.md) The Torah portion of the week, read in full with the same meticulous Hebrew narration. Conceived as a steady, listener‑friendly companion to the annual synagogue cycle, it serves both those who want to hear the text read beautifully and those who study it deeply.

[**Piyutim:**](media/Piyutim.md) Liturgical poetry and sacred song re‑imagined through AI‑generated multi‑track compositions and visual stem renderings. From timeless traditional melodies to original arrangements, the series treats the piyut as a living, breathing art form.

[**Aramaic Class:**](media/AramaicClass.md) A structured, hands‑on introduction to the language of the Talmud and Zohar, built around figurine‑based visual teaching and authentic Eastern pronunciation. It starts with the simplest nouns and patiently walks the learner all the way through to real reading competence.

## [GPS Hebrew Clock](HebrewClock.md)

A timepiece that tells the hour the ancient way — dividing daylight and darkness into twelve proportional parts, shifting with the seasons. More a conceptual instrument than a standard clock, it connects modern timekeeping to the temporal rhythm of Hebrew prayer and tradition. It's at once a practical tool, a curiosity, and a quiet reminder that time itself can be measured by a sacred calendar.

</section>

## Android Apps

[**HebrewDate:**](apps/HebrewDateAndroidApp.md) An application that displays the current Gregorian and Hebrew dates, including the Hebrew date in gematria and the day’s holiday, along with a browsable Hebrew month calendar. It offers home-screen widgets for the date, a monthly calendar, and an analog clock that can show halachic times, as well as an optional ongoing notification containing the current date. The clock’s time definitions can be configured through preset or custom opinions for dawn, misheyakir, and nightfall.

[**Psalms:**](apps/PsalmsAndroidApp.md) An application for reading the book of Psalms, presenting each psalm with its title and description, verse numbers in Hebrew, and the text arranged in poetic lines. It can play audio recordings of the psalms from two downloadable sets—a cloned voice and an original recording—stored locally on the device, and during playback it highlights the individual word being spoken.

[**Scriptures:**](apps/ScripturesAndroidApp.md) Torah, Prophets, Writings, and the core texts of the Oral Law in a single Android app. The Institute's full text corpus, navigable, with attention to RTL typography.

</section>

## Booklets Series

[**Halakhah, Haggadah, and History:**](HalakhahHaggadahHistory.md) A growing library of booklets that weaves legal rulings, narrative legends, and historical context into a single, flowing read. Each volume takes one holiday or practice — Pesach, Hanukkah, Lag BaOmer — and traces its journey from biblical roots through rabbinic discussion and into living custom, always anchored in primary sources.

**Weekly Parashah:** A clean, print‑ready companion for each week's Torah portion, designed to sit alongside the Institute's Weekly Parashah video series. It gathers the Hebrew text, a faithful translation, and a curated selection of commentary, all in a format meant for the Shabbat table or a quiet study session.

**Zaggin diDhava (Bells of Gold):** A small, jewel‑like volume whose Aramaic title promises something precious. Without giving away its contents, it's the kind of booklet the Institute does best: a tightly focused exploration where language, source‑text, and design meet — part anthology, part commentary, and entirely a delight for the attentive reader.
</section>

## Libraries

Free software code packages released by us — reusable building blocks that power the apps, web, and products.

[**Scriptures:**](https://github.com/ReubenInstitute/Scriptures) Hebrew scripture text classes (Bible, Mishnah, Talmud, Zohar) for navigating the Hebrew text corpus, with a bundled web viewer for browsing the texts.

[**Date:**](https://github.com/SciNova/Date) Gregorian date/time utilities (Date, Time, DateTime) for Gregorian calendar and Julian day calculations.

[**Hebrew:**](https://github.com/ReubenInstitute/Hebrew) Hebrew text and numeral utilities — letter constants, transliteration helpers, and Hebrew numeral (gematria) conversion.

[**HebrewDate:**](https://github.com/ReubenInstitute/HebrewDate) Hebrew calendar utilities — dates, holidays, and Torah reading schedule.

[**Astro:**](https://github.com/SciNova/Astro) Astronomy utilities for celestial location, and sun/moon position calculation.

**HebrewYemama:** Halakhic day-division times (yemama), clock rendering, and custom day-definitions.
</section>
